Sweet Breakfast

What You'll Need

Main Ingredients

Thick-sliced bread — 2 slices

Eggs — 2

Milk — 2 tablespoons

Strawberry jam — 1 tablespoon

Peanut butter — 1 tablespoon

Toppings & Extras

Butter — 1 small piece (for frying)

Honey or condensed milk — to taste

Fresh strawberries — 2 to 3 (optional)

Kitchen Tools

  • Frying pan → Cooking the toast
  • Shallow bowl → Whisking the eggs
  • Butter knife → Spreading the jam and peanut butter
