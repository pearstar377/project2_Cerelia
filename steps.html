Sweet Breakfast

How to Make It

  1. Add the Filling

    Spread peanut butter on one slice and strawberry jam on the other. Press them together.

  2. Make the Egg Mix

    Crack 2 eggs into a shallow bowl, add the milk, and whisk until smooth.

  3. Dip the Sandwich

    Dip both sides and all edges of the sandwich into the egg mix so it soaks in.

  4. Fry Low and Slow

    Melt butter in the pan over medium-low heat. Fry each side for about 2 minutes until golden and crispy.

  5. Serve and Enjoy

    Cut it in half, drizzle with honey, add fresh strawberries, and eat it while it's warm!

Helpful Tips

  • Keep the heat low, or the outside will burn before the inside warms up.
  • Cut off the crusts for an extra soft bite.
  • Don't spread the jam to the edges, so it won't leak while frying.
